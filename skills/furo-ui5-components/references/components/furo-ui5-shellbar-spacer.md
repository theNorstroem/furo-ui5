# furo-ui5-shellbar-spacer

> Flexible spacer for distributing space in ShellBar.

**Class:** `FuroUi5ShellBarSpacer`
**Import:** `import "@furo/ui5/shellbar-spacer"`
**Extends:** `ShellBarSpacer`
**Category:** PageStructure

**Related:** [`furo-ui5-shellbar`](furo-ui5-shellbar.md), [`furo-ui5-toolbar-spacer`](furo-ui5-toolbar-spacer.md)

## Overview

### Overview
The `furo-ui5-shellbar-spacer` is an element, used for visual separation between the two content parts of the `furo-ui5-shellbar`.
**Note:** The `furo-ui5-shellbar-spacer` component is in an experimental state and is a subject to change.
